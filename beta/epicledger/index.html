Section 2

The real estate tax break

Goal 2: a second property's depreciation, and whether the write-off hits this year or waits.

How it works, and how to get it now instead of later

No sliders here — the mechanism, in three steps, then the three ways around the income cap.

Step 1

You buy a rental house. Not the one you live in — has to be a place you rent out.

→
Step 2

You pay for a report on the house. It unlocks a big write-off on this year's taxes.

→
Step 3

Check your income. That decides if you use the write-off now, or it waits.

If you make under $150,000

You use it right now — it lowers this year's paycheck tax bill.

If you make $150,000+

It waits. Nothing's lost — it banks up and pays out in full the year you sell the house.

Either way the write-off is real — it's only ever a question of now or later.

Way 3

Already own a rental making money? This write-off can cancel out that income — no matter what you make at your job.

Fixing-and-flipping houses also counts toward Way 2's hours — often faster than managing rentals. But the flip itself doesn't get this write-off: no depreciation, ordinary income plus a 15.3% extra tax, no 1031.

Real mechanics included: qualified contributions are pre-tax (the tax refund recycles into next year's pool, but contributions cap at the IRS limit and inflate future RMDs); the policy pays the real premium expense charge (7% / 6% / 2% by policy year); paying the house off frees the whole payment into the pool; and 0% business-credit stacking lands the first five years of chunks early. The Section 3 policy — tax-free filler

Tax-free policy loans at Section 3's loan rate. The cash value keeps earning the crediting rate the entire time — even on the loaned amount (index loan) — so the longer it's left, the more it can lend. The policy is never allowed to lapse: new loans stop at 90% of cash value, and if drift ever pushes the loan toward the cash value, the plan pays loan interest in cash to hold it — a lapse would make every prior loan retroactively taxable, so the model treats it as forbidden, not just unlucky.
