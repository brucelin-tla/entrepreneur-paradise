# Novice Playtest Protocol

**Why this exists:** DESIGN.md's legibility guardrail — "if a player can't tell *why*
something worked, the 'aha' is lost" — can't be tested by the owner (he can't un-know
finance) or by bots (they don't feel confusion). It needs genuinely finance-novice humans.
Target: 3–5 testers, ideally Team Life Academy clients/prospects who have NOT been through
coaching yet.

## The one rule that makes this work

**After every in-game month, the tester says out loud WHY they think their move worked
(or didn't).** That's the whole instrument. Three kinds of answer:

- ✅ **Correct why** ("the policy loan is tax-free, that's why my income jumped") — the
  lesson landed. This is the product working.
- 🟡 **Wrong why** ("I got lucky" / "revenue went up because I clicked more") — the
  mechanic worked but the teaching failed. Log it: this is a legibility bug, same severity
  as a display-truth bug.
- 🔴 **No why** ("no idea, the number went green") — the aha is fully lost. Log the exact
  screen + month.

## Session format (~45 min, remote or in person)

1. **Setup (5 min):** beta link on THEIR phone (the real funnel surface). No coaching, no
   explaining the game beyond what the game itself says. Screen-share or sit beside.
2. **Play (30 min):** they play; you stay silent except the one prompt after each month:
   *"Why do you think that worked?"* Never answer their questions — note them instead
   (every question they ask is something the game failed to surface).
3. **Debrief (10 min):** three questions —
   - "Explain, like I'm your friend, what a policy loan / good debt is." (Did the crown
     jewel concept transfer?)
   - "What was the most confusing moment?"
   - "Would you keep playing tonight if I hadn't asked you to?" (fun check — edutainment
     needs both.)

## Logging

One row per 🟡/🔴 moment: month, screen/panel, what they said, what the game had actually
told them. Drop rows into a "novice-playtest" note or straight into CLAUDE.md Pending work.
Patterns across 2+ testers = fix before the next promote; single-tester stumbles = watch list.

## Recruitment message (copy/paste, adjust names)

> Subject: 30 minutes to break my game?
>
> Hey [name] — I built something I think you'll enjoy: a phone game where you run a
> business for 36 months and try to end up wealthy AND free. It secretly teaches the
> real money strategies I work with every day, but here's the thing — I know this stuff
> too well to tell if the game actually *teaches* it, or just makes sense to me.
>
> That's where you come in. I need someone smart who hasn't been through my coaching to
> play it for 30 minutes while thinking out loud. You can't do it wrong — literally every
> place you get confused is a bug I need to find, so the more you stumble, the more you
> help. I'll take notes, you play.
>
> Got 45 minutes this week or next? I'll send the link. — Bruce
